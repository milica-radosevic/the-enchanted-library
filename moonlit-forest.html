About the Book

by Liora Woods

Deep within an ancient forest lies a hidden path that appears only beneath the light of the full moon.

When Evelyn follows the mysterious trail between the trees, she discovers a forgotten world filled with glowing flowers, whispering leaves and creatures that seem to know her name. The deeper she travels, the more she begins to uncover the secrets of the forest and the reason the path chose her.

But when the moon begins to fade, Evelyn must find her way home before the enchanted forest changes forever.
