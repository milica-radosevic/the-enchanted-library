About the Book

by Rowan Vale

For centuries, dragons have been nothing more than legends told around warm fires. But deep beyond the mountains, one dragon still remains.

When Alden discovers the hidden creature, he learns that the last dragon is guarding an ancient secret that could change the fate of the kingdom. Together, they set out across forgotten valleys, ruined castles and dangerous lands in search of the truth.

As old enemies awaken and the kingdom begins to fall into chaos, Alden must decide whether some legends are meant to remain forgotten — or brought back to life.
